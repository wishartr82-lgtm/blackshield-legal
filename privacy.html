Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Contact: wishartr82@gmail.com

Blackshield is a personal tabletop roster and army list helper. This page describes what the software actually does today.

We do not have user accounts

Blackshield does not create logins, profiles, or an account system. You do not register with us to use the app.

Lists and settings stay on your device

Army lists, display preferences, and a local Pro unlock record are stored in your browser or app storage on the device (localStorage). We do not operate a cloud backup of your lists. If you export a list as JSON, that file stays wherever you save or share it.

What we collect only if you send it

An optional “Send a suggestion” form can send your written message and, if you type one, a reply email to the developer inbox (wishartr82@gmail.com) through our hosting email provider. Do not send passwords or payment details in that form. If you do not use the form, we do not receive that information.

Purchases

Blackshield is a paid unlock (monthly or lifetime). On Android, purchases go through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and ties it to your Google account. We store a local unlock record on the device so the app can remember that you paid. We do not see your full card number.

On the website or desktop build, checkout may use PayPal (and, if configured, other checkout links). Those providers process the payment under their own privacy policies. We may store a local unlock record and, if the provider returns it, an email address associated with the purchase.

What we do not run

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, crash reporters, or social logins. The Android build requests Internet access so it can load bundled rules data and talk to Google Play for purchases.

Fonts and rules data

The web pages may load fonts from Google Fonts. Rules text in the app comes from publicly available reference pages stored as local JSON. That is game-reference data, not personal data.

Children

Blackshield is meant for tabletop players, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Clear the app or site storage to delete lists and the local unlock record on that device.
  • Email wishartr82@gmail.com if you want a suggestion or reply-to address removed from the inbox.
  • Manage Android purchases and subscriptions in the Google Play Store.

Changes

If the app starts collecting something new, we will update this page and the date above.
